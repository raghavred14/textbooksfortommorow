How to Donate Books

  • 1. Gather your books

    Look for review books, textbooks, and gently used fiction you no longer need.

  • 2. Reach out

    Email or call us and we'll set up a drop-off or pickup in the Frisco / Dallas–Fort Worth area, or catch us at our next drive.

  • 3. We sort and ship

    Our volunteers catalog every book and send it to students locally and abroad.
