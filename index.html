Who we are

We are Textbooks for Tomorrow

At Textbooks for Tomorrow, we create supportive and inspiring spaces where curious young minds can learn and grow with us, 100% free. Because we love learning, we aim for more than great grades: we want to build confident, creative thinkers and give them an education that matters for their future, so students from every background can make an impact on the world.

We're a high school learning resource focused on distributing textbooks and sharing proven study guides. Our students leave with the character and confidence to make their mark, plus the knowledge and real-world skills to get ahead in whatever field they choose.

What we do

Three ways we help students

♻️

Reusing Textbooks

We collect gently used textbooks and review books and get them to students who can't afford them, both locally and around the world.

🗂️

Free Curriculum

We share proven study guides and learning resources so any student can prepare for class, AP exams, and standardized tests.

🧑‍🏫

Kids Tutoring Kids

Student volunteers tutor younger students. Teaching others helps both of them learn.

Donations

Give a book. Change a future.

Textbooks: We accept any kind of review textbook, including Princeton Review, Barron's, and official AP review books, plus fiction for our younger readers.

Other books: We accept gently used adult fiction and school-age books to send to imaginative readers around the world.

Money: Want to donate to us directly? Every dollar helps us get more books to more kids.
