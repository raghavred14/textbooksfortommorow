We are Textbooks for Tomorrow

We create supportive, inspiring spaces where curious young minds can learn and grow with us, 100% free. We want to build confident, creative thinkers and give students from every background an education that matters for their future.

We started in Frisco, Texas and now have chapters in Texas and Illinois. More than 1,100 high school volunteers have helped us collect over 12,000 books for students in 7 countries.

Our Mission Meet the Team

Featured Drive

Richwoods Book Drive

In fall 2025, about 120 volunteers fanned out across the Richwoods neighborhood in Frisco. Together we reached 1,600 houses and collected 1,836 books for students who need them.

Afterward, everyone stuck around for basketball, volleyball, and free food. Big thanks to our officers and to our local sponsors who made it possible.

What We Do

Book Drives

Door-to-door drives where teams of volunteers collect textbooks, review books, and fiction, then sort and ship them to students who need them.

Fundraising Events

Sports events and charity tournaments in volleyball, basketball, and pickleball. Entry fees go straight to buying and shipping books.

Community Engagement

High schoolers working as a team, earning service hours, and leading drives in their own neighborhoods.

Political & Educational Involvement

Pushing for access to education beyond our drives, including work on a bill to support students and schools.
