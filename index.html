We are Textbooks for Tomorrow

We create supportive, inspiring spaces where curious young minds can learn and grow with us, 100% free. We want to build confident, creative thinkers and give students from every background an education that matters for their future.

We're a high school learning resource focused on distributing textbooks and sharing proven study guides. Our book drives, tournaments, and tutoring help students walk away with the knowledge, skills, and confidence to make their mark.

Our Mission Meet the Team

Featured Drive

Richwoods Book Drive

In fall 2025, about 120 volunteers fanned out across the Richwoods neighborhood in Frisco. Together we reached 1,600 houses and collected 1,836 books for students who need them.

Afterward, everyone stuck around for basketball, volleyball, and free food. Big thanks to our officers and to our local sponsors who made it possible.

What We Do

Charity Tournaments

We host volleyball, basketball, and pickleball tournaments around Frisco and Plano. Entry fees go straight to buying and shipping books, and winning teams take home cash prizes.

Upcoming Events →

  • Reusing Textbooks

    We collect gently used textbooks and review books, sort them, and get them to students who can't afford them, both locally and around the world.

  • Free Curriculum

    We share proven study guides and learning resources so any student can prepare for class, AP exams, and standardized tests.

  • Kids Tutoring Kids

    Student volunteers tutor younger students. Teaching others helps both of them learn, and volunteers earn service hours.
