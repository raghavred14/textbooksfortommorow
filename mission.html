Our Mission

Helping underprivileged students reach their full potential

Why we exist

Learning shouldn't depend on what you can afford

Textbooks and test-prep books are expensive, and lots of students go without them. Meanwhile, perfectly good review books pile up on shelves once a class or exam is over.

Textbooks for Tomorrow connects those two problems. We collect gently used books, sort them, and get them to students who need them, here in North Texas and around the world. Everything we offer is 100% free.

Mission statement

To help underprivileged students reach their full potential by reusing textbooks, offering free curriculum, and kids tutoring kids.

Reuse

Every donated book gets another life. We accept Princeton Review, Barron's, official AP review books, and gently used fiction and school-age books.

Free Curriculum

Proven study guides and resources that any student can use, no matter where they live or what school they go to.

Kids Tutoring Kids

Older students mentor and tutor younger ones, which builds confidence on both sides of the table.

What We Believe

  • Equal opportunity

    Every student, from every background, deserves rewarding and stimulating learning that prepares them for a full social and cultural life.

  • Confidence and creativity

    We want to build thinkers, not just test-takers, with the character to make their mark on the world.

  • Relevant education

    Knowledge and real-world skills that set students up for whatever field they go into.

  • Students leading students

    We're run by students. We know what learners need because we're learners too.
