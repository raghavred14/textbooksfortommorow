Who We Are

Textbooks for Tomorrow is run entirely by high school students. We started in Frisco, Texas and now have chapters in Texas and Illinois. Our officers plan the drives, run the tournaments, and handle every box of books from pickup to delivery.

Behind them are more than 1,100 student volunteers across our chapters in Texas and Illinois, who go door to door, sort books, and tutor younger kids.
