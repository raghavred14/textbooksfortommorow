Book Drive · Fall 2025

Richwoods Book Drive

About 120 volunteers went door to door across Frisco's Richwoods neighborhood. We reached 1,600 houses and collected 1,836 books, all headed to students who need them.

Afterward we celebrated with basketball, volleyball, and free food. Thank you to our officers, our volunteers, and Curry Pizza House and Far East Pizza Co. for supporting the drive.

Fundraising

Charity Tournaments

Our tournaments bring the community together and raise money for books. Entry fees go straight to buying and shipping books, and winning teams take home a cash prize.

At our basketball tournament, Team Trog took home the $250 prize. Congrats!
